# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — Surface and Grand Slam balance pass

- Rebalanced grass outcomes so declared grass specialists win a majority without monopolizing the season.
- Added explicit preferred-surface and natural style/surface synergy to match strength.
- Reduced generic grass bonuses for serve-heavy styles, preventing style alone from overriding surface identity.
- Increased the effect of the total matchup advantage on service games.
- Added tournament-tier variance: Grand Slams are more merit-driven, Masters events moderately noisy, and 500/250 events more volatile.
- Added elite competitive-repeatability modifiers to separate Generational, Legend and Epic five-year Slam ceilings.
- Weighted preferred-surface generation so serve-and-volley, big-server and all-court players produce plausible grass specialists while retaining awkward combinations.
- Added a deterministic Monte Carlo balance suite covering grass dominance, participation-adjusted win rates, champion variety and five-year Slam ceilings.
